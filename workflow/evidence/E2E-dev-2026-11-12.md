**Follow-up defect:** an AI answer can be lost when the page refreshes while the call is in flight.
- The server has already finished the call and counted it against the quota, but the panel remounts and shows nothing.
- It was seen 3 times in 7 runs (runs 3, 4 and 6), each time when the click came within a few seconds of another action on the same page.
- A real user is less likely to hit it than a script, but it wastes quota when they do.
