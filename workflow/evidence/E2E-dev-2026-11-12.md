## Defect found and fixed: a QA draft lost during a test save

**Cause:** the draft panel was remounted, through its React `key`, whenever the recorded tests changed. A draft requested while **Save test** was in flight ran after the save, then was discarded by the remount after its quota was spent.

**Fix (#41):**
- The draft now returns `basedOn`, the tests it used.
- The panel shows it only while those match the tests on screen, and hides it as stale otherwise.
- The panel is no longer remounted.

**Evidence:** a script that clicks **Draft** immediately after **Save test**.

| Build | Draft shown | Stale draft hidden after another test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deployed dev (old code) | no | yes |
| Fixed build, run locally against the dev database and LLM | yes, 3 out of 3 | yes, with a note, 3 out of 3 |

**Unit tests:** `testsKey` and `draftView` in `src/lib/qa/feedback-draft.test.ts`.
